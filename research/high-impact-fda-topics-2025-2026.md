## 1. HIGH-PROFILE FDA ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS

### 1A. GLP-1 Compounding Crackdown (Semaglutide/Tirzepatide)
- **What happened:** FDA issued 50+ warning letters to compounders and 30 to telehealth companies marketing compounded GLP-1 drugs (September-December 2025). Threatened "legal action without further notice."
- **Product type:** Pharma/compounding
- **Why it matters broadly:** Signals FDA willingness to aggressively pursue telehealth and compounding operations.
- **Search interest:** EXTREMELY HIGH.

### 1B. FreshRealm / Listeria Outbreak (Chicken Fettuccine Alfredo)
- **What happened:** Multi-state listeria outbreak linked to FreshRealm-produced prepared pasta meals sold at Walmart, Trader Joe's, Kroger, Albertsons, and Sprouts. 28 confirmed cases, 27 hospitalizations, 7 deaths. Recall expanded from June through November 2025.
- **Product type:** Food (prepared/RTE meals)
- **Why it matters broadly:** Deadliest foodborne illness outbreak of the year. Highlights supplier ingredient testing failures and co-manufacturing liability.
- **Search interest:** HIGH.

### 1C. ByHeart Infant Formula Botulism Outbreak
- **What happened:** First-ever known infant botulism outbreak linked to infant formula globally. 28 confirmed + 20 probable cases. Full product recall November 2025. FDA issued warning letters to 4 major retailers for failing to remove recalled product.
- **Product type:** Food (infant formula)
- **Why it matters broadly:** Historic first. Established new FDA precedent for retailer accountability in recall execution.
- **Search interest:** VERY HIGH.

### 1D. CDER Warning Letters 50% Spike
- Warning letters from CDER rose 50% in FY 2025. 22% targeted telehealth platforms. Over 200 enforcement letters challenging drug advertising/promotion.

### 1E. Kratom/Tianeptine Crackdown
- HHS/FDA crackdown on kratom-related products (7-hydroxymitragynine) found in energy drinks, gummies, and supplements at gas stations and convenience stores.

### 1F. Retailer Warning Letters for Recall Failures
- December 2025 -- FDA issued warning letters to 4 major retailers for failing to remove recalled ByHeart infant formula. First aggressive FDA action against retailers for recall execution.

## 2. ACTIVE INGREDIENT CONTROVERSIES

### 2A. Synthetic Food Dyes -- Full Phase-Out by End of 2027

| Dye | Status | Deadline |
|-----|--------|----------|
| FD&C Red No. 3 | **BANNED** (revoked Jan 15, 2025) | Jan 15, 2027 (food) / Jan 18, 2028 (drugs) |
| FD&C Red No. 40 | Voluntary phase-out requested | End of 2027 (target) |
| FD&C Yellow No. 5 | Voluntary phase-out requested | End of 2027 (target) |
| FD&C Yellow No. 6 | Voluntary phase-out requested | End of 2027 (target) |
| FD&C Blue No. 1 | Voluntary phase-out requested | End of 2027 (target) |
| FD&C Blue No. 2 | Voluntary phase-out requested | End of 2027 (target) |
| FD&C Green No. 3 | Voluntary phase-out requested | End of 2027 (target) |

- ~40% of packaged food supply has pledged near-term removal.
- FDA approved 3 new natural food dye alternatives (May 2025).
- **Search interest:** EXTREMELY HIGH.

### 2B. GRAS Reform / "Closing the GRAS Loophole"
- Proposed rule for mandatory GRAS submission now at White House review stage.
- **Search interest:** HIGH among food ingredient companies.

### 2C. NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide) -- Reinstated
- FDA reversed NMN exclusion (September 2025) after NPA lawsuit. Now lawful but requires NDI notification.

### 2D. Formaldehyde in Hair Straightening/Smoothing Products
- Six missed NPRM deadlines (Oct 2023 through Dec 2025). No new timeline.

### 2E. PFAS ("Forever Chemicals") in Food Packaging
- All PFAS grease-proofing agents voluntarily withdrawn. FDA revoked 35 food contact notifications (Jan 2025).
- 20+ states now have their own PFAS bans.
- **Search interest:** HIGH.

### 2F. Lead and Heavy Metals in Baby Food
- FDA finalized action levels for lead: 10 ppb (January 2025).
- Virginia passed mandatory testing + QR code labeling law (effective Jan 1, 2026).

### 2G. CBD/Hemp -- Major Legislative Change
- Section 781 (November 2025): redefining hemp by TOTAL THC, effective November 12, 2026.
- New cap: 0.4 mg total THC per product container.
- **Search interest:** VERY HIGH.
