Frito-Lay Issues Voluntary Allergy Alert on Undeclared Milk in Miss Vickie’s Spicy Dill Pickle Potato Chips

Frito-Lay has issued a voluntary allergy alert and recall for select 8 oz. bags of Miss Vickie’s Spicy Dill Pickle Potato Chips due to the potential presence of undeclared milk. The recall was initiated because some bags might contain jalapeño-flavored potato chips, which contain milk. Individuals with a milk allergy or sensitivity are at risk of a serious allergic reaction if they consume the product. The affected products were distributed in Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, New Mexico, Oklahoma, and Texas.
